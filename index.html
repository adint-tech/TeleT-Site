About TeleT LLC

Clinical expertise.
Regulatory precision.

TeleT LLC is a clinical research and regulatory affairs consultancy founded by Tabinda Ali, MBBS, MS, CCRC — a physician-scientist with dual academic specialization in Clinical Research Management and Regulatory Affairs, and years of hands-on experience at one of the nation's leading academic medical centers.

With a medical degree from Baqai Medical University and a Master of Clinical Research (4.0 GPA) from The Ohio State University, Tabinda brings an unusual combination of clinical fluency and regulatory rigor. Her career spans clinical trial management, IRB compliance, quality assurance, data coordination, and regulatory strategy across both academic and ambulatory settings.

TeleT LLC provides institutional-grade expertise to sponsors, sites, and organizations that need a trusted partner for clinical research operations and regulatory consulting.

What We Offer

Services & Capabilities

01

Regulatory Affairs Consulting

FDA submission strategy, IND applications, regulatory document management, life cycle planning, and compliance with federal guidance for drugs, biologics, and devices.

02

Clinical Trial Operations

Site activation, IRB submissions, protocol development, feasibility assessments, enrollment strategy, and full study lifecycle management from start-up through closeout.

03

IRB & Compliance

Preparation and submission of electronic IRB applications, protocol amendments, SAE reports, DSMB submissions, renewal documentation, and informed consent development.

04

Quality & Auditing

Risk-based monitoring, GCP compliance audits, SOP development, QAPI planning, corrective action plans, and preparation for regulatory surveys and state licensure.

05

Data Management

EDC setup and oversight using DataTrak and related systems, data quality assurance, source data management, CRF review, adverse event reporting, and data management plan development.

06

Medical Writing

Protocol development, informed consent documents, clinical study reports, regulatory briefing documents, and scientific content for research and submission purposes.
