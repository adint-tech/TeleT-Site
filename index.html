About TeleT LLC

Clinical expertise.
Regulatory precision.

TeleT LLC is a clinical research and regulatory affairs consultancy founded by Tabinda Ali, MBBS, MS, CCRC. She holds a medical degree from Baqai Medical University and a Master of Clinical Research from The Ohio State University, where she graduated with a 4.0 GPA.

Her work spans clinical trial management, IRB compliance, quality assurance, data coordination, and regulatory strategy across academic and ambulatory settings. TeleT LLC brings that experience to sponsors, sites, and research organizations.

What We Offer

Services & capabilities

Support across the full research lifecycle, from the first strategy session through final closeout.

01

Regulatory Affairs Consulting

FDA submission strategy, IND applications, and regulatory document management for drugs, biologics, and devices.

02

Clinical Trial Operations

Site activation, protocol development, feasibility assessment, and study management from start-up through closeout.

03

IRB & Compliance

IRB applications, protocol amendments, SAE and DSMB reporting, and informed consent development.

04

Quality & Auditing

Risk-based monitoring, GCP audits, SOP development, and corrective action planning.

05

Data Management

EDC setup and oversight, source data management, CRF review, and data quality assurance.

06

Medical Writing

Protocols, informed consent documents, clinical study reports, and regulatory briefing documents.
