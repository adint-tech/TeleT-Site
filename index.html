TeleT LLC is a clinical research and regulatory affairs consultancy founded by Tabinda Ali, MBBS, MS, CCRC. She holds a medical degree from Baqai Medical University and a Master of Clinical Research from The Ohio State University, where she graduated with a 4.0 GPA.

Her work covers clinical trial management, IRB compliance, quality assurance, data coordination, and regulatory strategy in academic and ambulatory settings. Through TeleT LLC, she offers that experience to sponsors, sites, and research organizations.

Services

Support across the full research lifecycle, from the first strategy session through final closeout.
