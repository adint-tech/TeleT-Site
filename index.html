About TeleT LLC

Clinical expertise.
Regulatory precision.

TeleT LLC is a clinical research and regulatory affairs consultancy founded by Tabinda Ali, MBBS, MS, CCRC — a physician-scientist with dual academic specialization in Clinical Research Management and Regulatory Affairs, and years of hands-on experience at one of the nation's leading academic medical centers.

With a medical degree from Baqai Medical University and a Master of Clinical Research (4.0 GPA) from The Ohio State University, Tabinda brings an unusual combination of clinical fluency and regulatory rigor. Her career spans oncology trial management, IRB compliance, quality assurance, data coordination, and regulatory strategy across both academic and ambulatory settings.

Currently serving as Portfolio Manager at Michigan Medicine's Clinical Trial Support Office, she manages the end-to-end lifecycle of complex multi-sponsor clinical trials — from activation and IRB submission through ongoing compliance, enrollment strategy, and closeout.

TeleT LLC was founded to bring that institutional-grade expertise to sponsors, sites, and organizations that need a trusted partner for clinical research operations and regulatory consulting.
